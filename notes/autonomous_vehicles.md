# Autonomous Vehicles – Detailed Notes

## 1. Introduction
Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) are vehicles capable of operating without human control using AI, sensors, and real-time decision systems.

---

## 2. Working Principle
1. Environment sensing
2. Data processing
3. Decision-making
4. Vehicle control

---

## 3. Technologies Used

### 3.1 Computer Vision
Used for object detection and lane tracking.

### 3.2 LiDAR
Creates 3D maps of surroundings.

### 3.3 Radar
Measures speed and distance of nearby objects.

### 3.4 GPS
Provides navigation and positioning.

---

## 4. Levels of Automation
- Level 0 → Manual driving
- Level 1 → Driver assistance
- Level 2 → Partial automation
- Level 3 → Conditional automation
- Level 4 → High automation
- Level 5 → Full automation

---

## 5. Applications
- Self-driving taxis
- Autonomous trucks
- Delivery robots
- Smart transportation

---

## 6. Advantages
- Reduced accidents
- Better fuel efficiency
- Reduced traffic congestion
- Improved accessibility

---

## 7. Challenges
- Ethical concerns
- Sensor limitations
- Cybersecurity threats
- Legal regulations

---

## 8. Future Scope
- AI traffic systems
- Connected smart cities
- Fully autonomous transportation

---

## 9. Career Scope
- Robotics Engineer
- AI Engineer
- Embedded Systems Developer

---

## 10. Conclusion
Autonomous Vehicles represent the future of transportation by combining AI, robotics, and real-time analytics to create safer and smarter mobility systems.
